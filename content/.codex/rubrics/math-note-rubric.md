# Math Note Rubric

Threshold: 85/100. Correctness below 25/30 requires revision or a warning.

- Correctness: 30
  - Definitions and claims are mathematically sound.
  - Proof steps do not hide essential hypotheses.
- Locality and preservation: 20
  - Edits stay within requested scope.
  - Existing useful content, links, examples, and numbering are preserved.
- Clarity: 20
  - The note has a clear local purpose.
  - Notation is introduced before use.
- Obsidian compatibility: 15
  - Markdown, wikilinks, callouts, and LaTeX are valid.
  - Inline math uses `$...$`; display math uses `$$...$$`.
- Source and uncertainty handling: 15
  - Nontrivial claims have a source, proof sketch, or uncertainty marker.

If the score is below threshold, revise only the weakest dimensions unless the user approves broader changes.
