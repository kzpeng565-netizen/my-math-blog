# Mathematical Article Structure Rubric

Threshold: 85/100.

- Main thread: 25
  - The reader can state the article's purpose after the introduction.
  - Each section advances the thread.
- Dependency order: 20
  - Definitions, notation, and lemmas appear before use.
  - Examples appear near the concepts they illuminate.
- Cognitive load: 20
  - Sections are neither overloaded nor fragmented.
  - Technical jumps are signposted.
- Mathematical integrity: 20
  - Claims are accurate and scoped.
  - Proof sketches do not overpromise.
- Blog readability: 15
  - Headings are informative.
  - The ending returns to the article's motivation.

For restructuring, first produce a proposed outline and a move map. Do not rewrite the article until the user approves.
