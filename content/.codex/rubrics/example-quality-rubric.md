# Example And Counterexample Rubric

Threshold: 88/100. Correctness below 28/35 requires rebuilding the example.

- Correctness: 35
  - The object satisfies all stated hypotheses.
  - For counterexamples, the conclusion genuinely fails.
- Relevance: 20
  - The example targets the user's concept or article section.
  - It reveals the mechanism being discussed.
- Verification: 15
  - The answer includes a concise check of hypotheses and conclusion.
- Pedagogical value: 15
  - The example is simple enough to teach from.
  - Complexity is justified when unavoidable.
- Note readiness: 15
  - The result can be pasted into an Obsidian note with minimal cleanup.
  - Notation and formulas are stable.

Preferred output for counterexamples:

```text
Claim being tested:
Counterexample:
Hypotheses check:
Conclusion failure:
Why this is minimal or natural:
Note-ready version:
Score:
```
