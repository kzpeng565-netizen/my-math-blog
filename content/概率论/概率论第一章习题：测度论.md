> \end{align*}$$
> and $\textbf{upper semicontinuous}$ (u.s.c.) if $-f$ is $l$.s.c. Show that $f$ is $l$.s.c. if and only if $\{x : f(x) \leq a\}$ is closed for each $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and conclude that semicontinuous functions are measurable.

$$
\liminf _{y \to x}f(y):=\lim_{ \delta \to 0 } \inf \{ f(y)\mid0<|y-x|<\delta \}
$$
Part 1
如果函数满足极限下确界，那么$\{ x \mid f(x)>a \}$是一个开集（根据极限的保序性质）
Part 2
如果$\{ x\mid f(x)\leq a \}$是一个闭集，那么$\{ x \mid f(x)>a \}$是一个开集，任取一个点属于这个开集，总可以找到一个半径是r的开球（不包含$x_{0}$），每一个开球的点都满足$f(x)>a$

$$

>Let $g$ be an integrable function on $\mathbb{R}$ and $\epsilon > 0$. 
>(i) Use the definition of the integral to conclude there is a simple function $\varphi = \sum_k b_k 1_{A_k}$ with $\int |g - \varphi| \, dx < \epsilon$. 
>(ii) Use Exercise A.2.1 to approximate the $A_k$  by finite unions of intervals to get a step function
>$$\begin{align*}
> q = \sum_{j=1}^k c_j 1_{(a_{j-1},a_j)}
> \end{align*}$$
> with $a_0 < a_1 < \cdots < a_k$, so that $\int |g - q| < \epsilon$. 
> (iii) Round the corners of $q$ to get a continuous function $r$ so that $\int |q - r| \, dx < \epsilon$.

(i)
根据定义：假定$g\geq0$
$\int gd\mu=\sup\left\{  \int fd\mu\mid0\leq f\leq g, f\text{是一个有界函数且支撑集测度有限}  \right\}$
$\forall\varepsilon>0,\exists f,\int gd\mu\leq \int fd\mu+\varepsilon$，并且$\int gd\mu\geq \int fd\mu$
根据有界函数的积分定义，可以用简单函数的积分来逼近
$\int fd\mu=\sup_{\varphi\leq f}\int \varphi d\mu=\inf_{\psi\geq f}\int \psi d\mu$
因此：$\forall\varepsilon>0,\exists \varphi,\ \int fd\mu-\int \varphi d\mu\geq 0$，同理$\forall\varepsilon>0,\ \exists \psi, \ \int \psi d\mu-\int fd\mu>0$
分别代入$g^{+}和g^{-}$
$$
\begin{align}
 & \int g^{+}d\mu-\int \varphi d\mu \leq2\varepsilon \\
 & \int \psi d\mu-\int g^{-} d\mu\leq\varepsilon \\
\implies & \int|g^{+}-\varphi|+|\psi-g^{-}|\leq 3\varepsilon \\
\implies  & \int|g+\varphi'|d\mu\leq3\varepsilon
\end{align}
$$
