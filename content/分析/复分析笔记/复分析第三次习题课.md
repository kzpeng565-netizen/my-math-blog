### 证明思路补充
由于 $u$ 是调和的，有 $\Delta u = \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x^{2}} + \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial y^{2}} = 0$。这等价于：
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} \right) = -\frac{\partial}{\partial y} \left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} \right),
$$
即微分形式 $\omega = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} dx + \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} dy$ 是闭的（$d\omega = 0$）。由于 $\Omega$ 是单连通的，$\omega$ 是恰当的，因此存在函数 $v$ 使得 $dv = \omega$。

原文中给出了 $v$ 的积分表达式（固定点 $(x_0, y_0) \in \Omega$）：
$$
v(x,y) = \int_{(x_0,y_0)}^{(x,y)} -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} dx + \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} dy + C,
$$
其中 $C$ 是常数。由于积分与路径无关，可以选择特定路径简化计算，如原文所示：
$$
v(x,y) = \int_{x_0}^{x} -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(s, y_0)  ds + \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, t)  dt + C.
$$
这里，第一个积分沿水平路径从 $(x_0, y_0)$ 到 $(x, y_0)$，第二个积分沿垂直路径从 $(x, y_0)$ 到 $(x, y)$。

### 验证柯西-黎曼方程
现在验证 $v$ 满足柯西-黎曼方程。

首先，计算 $\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}$：
$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \left[ \int_{x_0}^{x} -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(s, y_0)  ds + \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, t)  dt + C \right].
$$
- 第一项不依赖于 $y$，导数为零。
- 第二项由微积分基本定理：$\frac{\partial}{\partial y} \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, t)  dt = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, y)$。

因此，
$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, y).
$$

其次，计算 $\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}$：
$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left[ \int_{x_0}^{x} -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(s, y_0)  ds + \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, t)  dt + C \right].
$$
- 第一项由微积分基本定理：$\frac{\partial}{\partial x} \int_{x_0}^{x} -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(s, y_0)  ds = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y_0)$。
- 第二项：$\frac{\partial}{\partial x} \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, t)  dt = \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left( \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x, t) \right) dt = \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial x^{2}}(x, t)  dt$.

所以，
$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y_0) + \int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial x^{2}}(x, t)  dt.
$$
由于 $u$ 是调和的，$\frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial x^{2}} = -\frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial y^{2}}$，代入得：
$$
\int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial x^{2}}(x, t)  dt = -\int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial y^{2}}(x, t)  dt.
$$
由微积分基本定理：
$$
-\int_{y_0}^{y} \frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial y^{2}}(x, t)  dt = -\left[ \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, t) \right]_{t=y_0}^{t=y} = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y) + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y_0).
$$
因此，
$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y_0) + \left( -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y) + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y_0) \right) = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}(x, y).
$$
综上，有：
$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}, \quad \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x},
$$
即 $v$ 满足柯西-黎曼方程，且 $v$ 是调和的（因为柯西-黎曼方程蕴含 $\Delta v = 0$）。

### 唯一性证明
假设存在另一个共轭调和函数 $v'$，满足相同的柯西-黎曼方程。令 $w = v - v'$，则：
$$
\frac{\partial w}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial v'}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} - (-\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}) = 0,
$$
$$
\frac{\partial w}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial v'}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = 0.
$$
因此 $w$ 在 $\Omega$ 上为常数，即 $v$ 在相差一个常数意义下唯一。证毕。
