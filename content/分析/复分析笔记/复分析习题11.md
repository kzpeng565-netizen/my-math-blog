# 11. 对数积分与边界行为

> [!Note] 11
> Show that if $|a|<1$, then
>
> $$
> \int_0^{2 \pi} \log \left|1-a e^{i \theta}\right| d \theta=0
> $$
> Then, prove that the above result remains true if we assume only that $|a| \leq 1$.

- 当$\left| a \right|<1$
设$f(z)=1-az$, 在$D_{1}(0)$内没有零点, 所以存在$h(z)$是全纯函数, 在单位圆盘上$e^{h(z)}=f(z)$ . $f(0)=1$, 我们可以取其中一支对数, 使得$h(0)=0$.
$$
\int_{0}^{2\pi} \log \left| 1-ae^{i\theta} \right|  \, d\theta=\int_{0}^{2\pi} \mathrm{Re}h(e^{i\theta}) \, d\theta  =\mathrm{Re}h(0)=0
$$
- 当$\left| a \right|=1$
法一
$$
\int_{0}^{2\pi} \log \left| 1-ae^{i\theta} \right|  \, d\theta=\int_{0}^{2\pi} \log \left| 1-e^{i(\theta+\alpha)} \right|  \, d\theta=\int_{0}^{2\pi} \log \left| 1-e^{i\theta} \right|  \, d\theta   
$$
$$
\begin{align}
\int_{0}^{2\pi} \log \left| 1-e^{i\theta} \right|  \, d\theta=  & 2\int_{0}^{\pi} \ln \left| 2-2\cos\theta \right|  \, d\theta \\
= & 4\left( \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln2+\ln(2\sin ^{2}\theta) \, d\theta  \right) \\
\end{align}
$$
设$I=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln \sin x \, dx=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln \cos x \, dx$
$$
2I+\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\ln2dx=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \ln \sin2x \, dx =\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\pi} \ln \sin x \, dx =I\implies I=-\frac{\pi}{2}\ln2
$$
​代入原式有积分等于0

法二
对于任意 $b \in \mathbb{C}$ 满足 $|b| \leq 1$，通过旋转总可设 $b$ 为实数且 $0 \leq b \leq 1$。
$$
|1 - b e^{i\theta}|^2 = 1 - 2b \cos\theta + b^2
$$
然后估计上下界, 以使用Lebesgue控制收敛
$$
1 - 2b \cos\theta + b^2 \leq (1+b)^2 \leq 4 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \log|1 - b e^{i\theta}| \leq \log 2
$$
$$
1 - 2b \cos\theta + b^2 = (1-b)^2 + 4b \sin^2(\theta/2) \geq 4b \sin^2(\theta/2)
$$

令$b \geq 1/2$，则 $|1 - b e^{i\theta}| \geq 2\sqrt{b} |\sin(\theta/2)| \geq \sqrt{2} |\sin(\theta/2)|$
从而有
$$
|\log|1 - b e^{i\theta}|| \leq \max\!\big( \log 2, \, -\tfrac{1}{2}\log 2 - \log|\sin(\theta/2)| \big) =: g(\theta).
$$
$g(\theta)$是可积的, 且与$b$无关, 所以积分与极限可以交换.

# 17. 最大模原理与像集包含

> [!Note] 17-a
> (a) Show that if $|f(z)|=1$ whenever $|z|=1$, then the image of $f$ contains the unit disc. [Hint: One must show that $f(z)=w_0$ has a root for every $w_0 \in \mathbb{D}$. To do this, it suffices to show that $f(z)=0$ has a root (why?). Use the maximum modulus principle to conclude.]

由于边界已经固定, 如果$f$不是常值函数, 根据最小模长原理一定有$\min_{z\in \mathbb{D}}\left| f(z) \right|=0$. 所以存在$z_{0}\in \mathbb{D},f(z_{0})=0$
因为$\left| f(z) \right|\equiv1$ $z\in \partial \mathbb{D}$, 所以对于任意$\left| w_{0} \right|<1$, 存在非常接近于圆盘边缘的$D_{r}(0)$使得$\min_{z\in D_{r}(0)}\left| f(z) \right|>\left| w_{0} \right|$ 
有Rouche 定理$f(z)-w_{0}$的零点个数与$f(z)$相同, 也就是至少存在一个零点

> [!Note] 17-b
> (b) If $|f(z)| \geq 1$ whenever $|z|=1$ and there exists a point $z_0 \in \mathbb{D}$ such that $\left|f\left(z_0\right)\right|<1$, then the image of $f$ contains the unit disc.

单位圆盘内一定存在$z_{1}$使得 $f(z_{1})=0$, 否则会在内部取到非零的极小模长点.
根据连续性, 同样存在圆$D_{r}(0)$, $\min \left| f(z) \right|>\left| w_{0} \right|$ 
# 18. 边值问题与唯一性

> [!Note] 18
> Show that there is no holomorphic function $f$ in the unit disc $\mathbb{D}$ that extends continuously to $\partial \mathbb{D}$ such that $f(z)=1 / z$ for $z \in \partial \mathbb{D}$.

设$f$是全纯的, $g(z)=zf(z)$, 当$\left| z \right|=1$时, $g(z)=1$.
[[复分析11 Conformal map#1.2.2 Boundary Properties|Conformal map]] $F(z)= \frac{i-z}{i+z}$ 是一个$\mathbb{H}\to \mathbb{D}$的共形映射, 并且把$\left| z \right|=1$ 映射到$\mathrm{Im}w=0$ 
从而 $g(F(z))=1$, $\forall z\in \mathbb{R}$, 根据Schwarz反射原理, $g(F(z))$可以延拓到$\mathbb{C}$上, 使得在实轴上存在相等的聚点, 因此$g(F(z))\equiv1$, 也就是$g(z)\equiv1$, 但$f(z)= \frac{1}{z}$并不是在单位圆盘内全纯的, 矛盾.
