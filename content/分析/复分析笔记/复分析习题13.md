> [!Note] 1.
> Prove that
>$$
> \Gamma(s)=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{n^s n!}{s(s+1) \cdots(s+n)}
>$$
> whenever $s \neq 0,-1,-2, \ldots$.
>[Hint: Use the product formula for $1 / \Gamma$, and the definition of the Euler constant $\gamma$.]

$$
\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)}=e^{\gamma s}\cdot \prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\left( 1+  \frac{s}{n} \right)e^{-\frac{s}{n}},\quad s\neq0,-1,-2,\dots
$$
$H_{N}=\sum_{n=1}^{N} \frac{1}{n},\:\gamma_{N}=\sum_{n=1}^{N} \frac{1}{n}-\log N$ 
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_N(s) & :=e^{\gamma_N s} \cdot s \prod_{n=1}^N\left(1+\frac{s}{n}\right) e^{-\frac{s}{n}} \\
& =e^{s \cdot \gamma_N} \cdot s \cdot\left(\prod_{n=1}^N\left(1+\frac{s}{n}\right)\right) \cdot \exp \left(-\sum_{n=1}^N \frac{s}{n}\right) \\
& =e^{s \cdot\left(\gamma_N-H_N\right)} \cdot s \cdot \prod_{n=1}^N\left(1+\frac{s}{n}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
> [!Note] 定理 1.7
>$$
>\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)}=e^{\gamma s}\cdot s \prod_{n=1}^{+\infty} \left( 1+ \frac{s}{n} \right)e^{-\frac{s}{n}}
>$$

所以
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Gamma(s) & =\lim _{N \rightarrow \infty} \frac{1}{P_N(s)} \\
& =\lim _{N \rightarrow \infty} \frac{e^{s \cdot\left(H_N-\gamma_N\right)}}{s \cdot \prod_{n=1}^N\left(1+\frac{s}{n}\right)} \\
& =\lim _{N \rightarrow \infty} \frac{e^{s\left(H_N-\gamma_N\right)}}{s \cdot \prod_{n=1}^N\left(\frac{n+s}{n}\right)}
\end{aligned}
$$
由于
$$
\prod_{n=1}^{N} \left(  \frac{n+s}{n} \right)= \frac{s(s+1)\dots(s+N)}{N!}
$$
且 $\gamma_{N}=H_{N}-\log N$
$$
\Gamma(s)=\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{n^s n!}{s(s+1) \cdots(s+n)}
$$

>whenever $s$ is a positive number. Show that if the left-hand side is interpreted as $\left(\Gamma^{\prime} / \Gamma\right)^{\prime}$, then the above formula also holds for all complex numbers $s$ with $s \neq 0,-1,-2, \ldots$.

\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)}=e^{\gamma s} \cdot s \prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(1+\frac{s}{n}\right) e^{-\frac{s}{n}},
$$
$$
\log \Gamma(s)=-\gamma s-\log s-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left[\log \left(1+\frac{s}{n}\right)-\frac{s}{n}\right] .

$$

\frac{d^2}{d s^2} \log \Gamma(s)=\frac{1}{s^2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(n+s)^2}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(s+n)^2},
