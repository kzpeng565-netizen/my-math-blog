#教材学习 #微积分 
## 教材内容

### 有限增量公式与泰勒公式
$$
f(a+h)=f(a)+\sum _{i}^{n} \frac{ \partial f }{ \partial x_{i} }(a+\theta h)h_{i} 
$$
$$
\theta \in[0,1],f\text{一阶可微}
$$
**泰勒公式**
$$
\begin{align}
 & f(a+h)=f(a)+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{k!}\left( h_{1}\frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x_{1} } +\dots+h_{m}\frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x_{m} }  \right)^{k}f(a) \\
 & +\frac{1}{n!}\left( \sum_{i}^{m}\frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x }h_{i}  \right) ^{n}f(a+\theta h)
\end{align}
$$
（上式的$f(a)$可以写在求和当中变为第0项）
或者类似于Peano余项有：
$$
f(a+h)=\sum ^{n}_{k=0} \frac{1}{k!}\left( \sum_{i}^{m}h_{i}\frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x }  \right)^{k}f(a)+o(\Vert{h}\Vert)
$$
如果使用重指数记号
$$
f(a+h)=\sum_{\left| \alpha \right| =0}^{n} \frac{1}{\alpha!}\partial^{\alpha}f(a)h^{\alpha}+R_{n+1}
$$
对于$f:\mathbb{R}^{m}\to \mathbb{R}$定义重指数$\alpha=(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\dots,\alpha_{m})$并且规定$\left| \alpha \right|=\alpha_{1}+\dots+\alpha_{m}$
同时：$\alpha! =\alpha_{1}!\alpha_{2}!\dots\alpha_{m}!$
$\partial^{\alpha}=\frac{ \partial^{\left| \alpha \right|} }{ \partial x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}\dots \partial x_{m}^{\alpha _{m}} }$
