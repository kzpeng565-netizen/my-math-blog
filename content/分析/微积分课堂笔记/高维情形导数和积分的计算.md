#考试 #微积分 

极坐标换元
$$
\begin{align}
x_{1}=r\cos \varphi_{1} \\
x_{2}=r\sin \varphi_{1}\cos \varphi_{2} \\
\vdots \\
x_{n-1}=r\sin \varphi_{1}\sin \varphi_{2}\dots \sin \varphi_{n-2}\cos \varphi_{n-1} \\
x_{n}=r\sin \varphi_{1} \sin \varphi_{2}\dots \sin \varphi_{n-1} 
\end{align}
$$
对于 $k=1,2, \ldots, n-2$ ，角度 $\varphi_k \in[0, \pi]$ 。最后一个角度 $\varphi_{n-1} \in[0,2 \pi)$ 。


$$
\mathbb{R}^{3}上\quad \left( \frac{1}{\left| x-y \right| } \right)'_{y_{1}}= \frac{x_{1}-y_{1}}{\left| x-y \right| ^{3}}\quad \left( \frac{1}{\left| x-y \right| } \right)'_{x_{1}}= \frac{y_{1}-x_{1}}{\left| x-y \right| ^{3}}
$$
而且$\frac{\left| x_{1}-y_{1} \right|}{\left| x-y \right|^{3}}\leq \frac{1}{\left| x-y \right|^{2}}$, 在$\mathbb{R}^{3}$中$\iiint_{B(y,r)}\frac{1}{\left| x-y \right|^{2}}dx=\iiint \frac{1}{r^{2}}r^{2}\sin \varphi_{1}dr d\varphi_{1}d\varphi_{2}=4\pi r$ 

常见的有源场
$$
\mathbf{E}=\frac{q}{4 \pi \epsilon_0} \frac{\mathbf{r}}{r^3}
$$
的标准函数是
$$
\frac{x}{\left| x \right| ^{3}},\quad \nabla \cdot \frac{x}{\left| x \right| ^{3}}=0
$$
