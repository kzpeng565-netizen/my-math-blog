> [!Note] 5
请发展例 3 的思路, 取 $\tilde{k}:=\sqrt{1-k^2},  \tilde{E}(k):=E(\tilde{k}),  \tilde{K}(k):=K(\tilde{k})$, 从而证明 (最早由勒让德给出):
a) $\frac{d}{dk}(E\tilde{K} + \tilde{E}K - K\tilde{K}) = 0;$
b) $E\tilde{K} + \tilde{E}K - K\tilde{K} = \frac{\pi}{2}.$


**a)**
证明 $\frac{d}{dk}(E\tilde{K} + \tilde{E}K - K\tilde{K}) = 0$
定义函数 $L(k) = E(k)\tilde{K}(k) + \tilde{E}(k)K(k) - K(k)\tilde{K}(k)$
椭圆积分的导数公式：
 $\frac{dK}{dk} = \frac{E(k) - (1 - k^2)K(k)}{k(1 - k^2)} = \frac{E - \tilde{k}^2 K}{k \tilde{k}^2}$（其中 $\tilde{k}^2 = 1 - k^2$）
 $\frac{dE}{dk} = \frac{E(k) - K(k)}{k}$
同时，考虑 $\tilde{k} = \sqrt{1 - k^2}$，故 $\frac{d\tilde{k}}{dk} = \frac{-k}{\tilde{k}}$。因此 $\frac{d\tilde{E}}{dk} = \frac{dE}{d\tilde{k}} \cdot \frac{d\tilde{k}}{dk} = \frac{\tilde{E} - \tilde{K}}{\tilde{k}} \cdot \frac{-k}{\tilde{k}} = \frac{-k (\tilde{E} - \tilde{K})}{\tilde{k}^2}$
$\frac{d\tilde{K}}{dk} = \frac{dK}{d\tilde{k}} \cdot \frac{d\tilde{k}}{dk} = \frac{\tilde{E} - (1 - \tilde{k}^2)K(\tilde{k})}{\tilde{k}(1 - \tilde{k}^2)} \cdot \frac{-k}{\tilde{k}}$
从而：
  $\frac{d\tilde{K}}{dk} = \frac{\tilde{E} - k^2 \tilde{K}}{\tilde{k} k^2} \cdot \frac{-k}{\tilde{k}} = \frac{- (\tilde{E} - k^2 \tilde{K})}{k \tilde{k}^2}$

计算 $L(k) = E\tilde{K} + \tilde{E}K - K\tilde{K}$ 的导数：

$$
\frac{dL}{dk} = \tilde{K} \left( \frac{dE}{dk} - \frac{dK}{dk} \right) + \tilde{E} \frac{dK}{dk} + K \frac{d\tilde{E}}{dk} + (E - K) \frac{d\tilde{K}}{dk}
$$

代入导数公式：
$\frac{dE}{dk} - \frac{dK}{dk} = \frac{E - K}{k} - \frac{E - \tilde{k}^2 K}{k \tilde{k}^2} = \frac{E}{k} \left(1 - \frac{1}{\tilde{k}^2}\right) = \frac{E}{k} \cdot \frac{-k^2}{\tilde{k}^2} = \frac{-k E}{\tilde{k}^2}$，所以：
  $$
  \tilde{K} \left( \frac{dE}{dk} - \frac{dK}{dk} \right) = \tilde{K} \cdot \frac{-k E}{\tilde{k}^2} = \frac{-k E \tilde{K}}{\tilde{k}^2}
  $$
整理可得
$$
\frac{dL}{dk} = \frac{1}{k \tilde{k}^2} \left[ -k^2 E \tilde{K} + \tilde{E} (E - \tilde{k}^2 K) - k^2 K (\tilde{E} - \tilde{K}) - (E - K)(\tilde{E} - k^2 \tilde{K}) \right]=0
$$

**b)** 
证明 $E\tilde{K} + \tilde{E}K - K\tilde{K} = \frac{\pi}{2}$
由 (a) 知 $\frac{dL}{dk} = 0$，故 $L(k)$ 为常数。只需计算 $k = 1$ 时的值
当 $k = 1$，$\tilde{k} = \sqrt{1 - 1} = 0$
$E(1) = \int_0^{\pi/2} \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 \theta}  d\theta = \int_0^{\pi/2} \cos \theta  d\theta = 1$
$K(1)$ 发散，但 $\tilde{E}(1) = E(0) = \int_0^{\pi/2} \sqrt{1 - 0}  d\theta = \frac{\pi}{2}$
$\tilde{K}(1) = K(0) = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{d\theta}{\sqrt{1 - 0}} = \frac{\pi}{2}$
代入 $L(k)$：
$$
L(1) = E(1)\tilde{K}(1) + \tilde{E}(1)K(1) - K(1)\tilde{K}(1) = 1 \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2} \cdot K(1) - K(1) \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{\pi}{2}
$$
因此，$L(k) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ 对于所有 $k$ 成立。

$$

证明
$\forall t\in(-\infty,+\infty)$, $F(t)=\int_{0}^{1} \frac{t}{x^{2}+t^{2}} f(x)\:dx$ ​有定义. 又任意$t_{0}\in(0,+\infty), g(x,t):= \frac{t}{t^{2}+x^{2}}f(x)$​	在$[0,1]\times\left[ \frac{t_{0}}{2}, 2t_{0} \right]$上连续, 所以$F(t)$在$t_{0}$处连续,
([[微积分04：含参变量常义积分#^72cdd7]] $F(t)$​的连续性需要一个区间的连续性来证明.)
所以在$(0,+\infty)$上连续. 同理在$(-\infty,0)$上也连续
对$t_{0}=0\:F(0)=0$, $\forall t\in(0,+\infty),\: F(t)=\left( \int_{0}^{t^{1/3}}+\int_{t^{1/3}}^{1} \right) \frac{t}{x^{2}+t^{2}}f(x)\:dx$
由积分中值定理 $\exists \xi\in[0,t ^{1/3}]\: s.t.$
$$
\int_{0}^{t^{1/3}} \frac{t}{x^{2}+t^{2}}f(x) \, dx =f(\xi)\int_{0}^{t^{1/3}} \frac{t}{x^{2}+t^{2}} \, dx =f(\xi)\arctan t^{-2/3}
$$
所以
$$
\lim_{t\to+0} \int_{0}^{t^{1/3}} \frac{t}{t^{2}+x^{2}}f(x) \, dx =f(0) \frac{\pi}{2}
$$
又因为
$$
\left\lvert  \int_{0}^{1} \frac{t}{t^{2}+x^{2}}f_{9}x \, dx   \right\rvert \leq max_{x\in[0,1]}\lvert f(x) \rvert  \frac{t}{t^{2/3 }+t^{2}}
$$
所以
$$
\lim_{t\to+0} \int_{t^{1/3}}^{1}  \frac{t}{t^{2}+x^{2}}f(x) \, dx =0\implies \lim_{t\to +0}F(t) = f(0) \frac{\pi}{2}
$$
同理$\lim_{t\to-0}F(t)=-f(0) \frac{\pi}{2}$
