> [!Note] 计算级数
>$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( 1+ \frac{1}{2}+\dots+\frac{1}{n} \right)x^{n} 
>$$

收敛域容易确定是$(-1,1)$
$$
\begin{align}
xS(x)=\sum_{n=1}\left( 1+\dots+\frac{1}{n} \right)x^{n+1} \\
=\sum_{n=1}\left( 1+\dots+ \frac{1}{n-1} \right)x^{n} \\
(1-x)S(x)=x+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n}x^{n}:=g(x) \\
g'(x)=\sum_{n=1}x^{n-1} = \frac{1}{1-x} \\
S(x)= \frac{1}{1-x}\int_{0}^{x} \frac{1}{1-t} \, dt= -\frac{\ln(1-x)}{1-x}  
\end{align}
$$​法二:
$S(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k}x^{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left( \sum_{n=k}^{\infty}x^{n} \right) \frac{1}{k}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k} \frac{x^{k}}{1-x}= \frac{1}{1-x}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^{k}}{x}$ 

法三:
$S(x)=\sum_{n=1}\left(  \frac{x}{1}x^{n-1}+\frac{x^{2}}{2}x^{n-2}+\dots+ \frac{x^{n}}{n} \right)=\left( \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^{n}}{n} \right)\left( \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^{n} \right)$ 

> [!Note] 2.
>证明：$\forall t \in \mathbb{R}, \int_0^{2 \pi} \mathrm{e}^{t \cos \theta} \cos (t \sin \theta) \mathrm{d} \theta=2 \pi$ ．

​	证 设

$$
f(t)=\int_0^{2 \pi} \mathrm{e}^{t \cos \theta} \cos (t \sin \theta) \mathrm{d} \theta, \quad t \in \mathbb{R}
$$


显然 $f(0)=2 \pi$ ．又由含参积分的微分法和格林公式可知

$$
\begin{aligned}
f^{\prime}(t) & =\int_0^{2 \pi} \mathrm{e}^{t \cos \theta} \cos \theta \cos (t \sin \theta) \mathrm{d} \theta-\int_0^{2 \pi} \mathrm{e}^{t \cos \theta} \sin (t \sin \theta) \sin \theta \mathrm{d} \theta \\
& =\oint_{\substack{x^2+y^2=1 \\
\text { 逆时针方向 }}} \mathrm{e}^{t x} \cos (t y) \mathrm{d} y+\mathrm{e}^{t x} \sin (t y) \mathrm{d} x \\
& =\iint_{x^2+y^2 \leqslant 1}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\mathrm{e}^{t x} \cos (t y)\right)-\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\mathrm{e}^{t x} \sin (t y)\right)\right] \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{~d} y \\
& =\iint_{x^2+y^2 \leqslant 1} 0 \mathrm{~d} x \mathrm{~d} y=0, \quad \forall t \in \mathbb{R},
\end{aligned}
$$
