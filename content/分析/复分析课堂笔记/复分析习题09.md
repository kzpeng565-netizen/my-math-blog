# 1. 单位圆盘上的调和函数
> [!Note] 1
> Prove that the function $u$ defined by
> 
> $$
> u(x, y)=\operatorname{Re}\left(\frac{i+z}{i-z}\right) \quad \text { and } \quad u(0,1)=0
> $$
> 
> is harmonic in the unit disc and vanishes on its boundary. Note that $u$ is not bounded in $\mathbb{D}$.

Since $\mathbb{D}$ is simply connected and $f(z)=\frac{i+z}{i-z}$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$, we have $u(x,y)=\mathrm{Re}f$ is harmonic on $\mathbb{D}$.
Let $z=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$, we can compute $u(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)= \frac{1-\cos ^{2}\theta-\sin ^{2}\theta}{\cos ^{2}\theta+(1-\sin\theta)^{2}}=0,\quad\text{when }\theta\neq \frac{\pi}{2}$ 
Since $u(0,1)=0$, $u(x,y)$ vanishes on its whole boundary.

**Remark** 如果沿着虚轴趋近于$(0,1)$, 则函数值应该趋于无穷, 因此这个函数只是"沿着边界连续", 而并非是在$\bar{\mathbb{D}}$上连续
# 2. Schwarz引理的应用
> [!Note] 2
> Show that if $f: D(0, R) \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is holomorphic, with $|f(z)| \leq M$ for some $M>0$, then
> 
> $$
> \left|\frac{f(z)-f(0)}{M^2-\overline{f(0)} f(z)}\right| \leq \frac{|z|}{M R} .
> $$
> 
> [Hint: Use the Schwarz lemma.]

Note that $M^{2}-\overline{f(0)}f(z)=0\iff f(z)=f(0),\left| f(z) \right|=M$, by Maximum modulo theorem, for f is not constant, the denominator can't be 0. (If $f(z)\equiv C$, $\forall M>C$, the inequality is trival )

Let $\psi_{\alpha}(z)= \frac{z-\alpha}{1-\bar{\alpha}z}$, $g(z)= \frac{f(z)}{M},g(z):D(0,R)\to \bar{\mathbb{D}}$
$h(z):=\psi_{g(0)}\left( g\left( \frac{z}{R} \right) \right)$, $h(z):\mathbb{D}\to \overline{\mathbb{D}}$ , $h(z)$ is holomorphic
From Maximum modulo theorem, for any interior point, $|h(z)|\neq1$, hence $h:\mathbb{D}\to \mathbb{D}$.
Note that $h(0)=\psi_{g(0)}(g(0))=0$, $\left| h \right|\leq \left| z \right|\implies$ the inequality holds
# 3. 伪双曲距离与Schwarz-Pick引理
> [!Note] 3-a
> The pseudo-hyperbolic distance between two points $z, w \in \mathbb{D}$ is defined by
> 
> $$
> \rho(z, w)=\left|\frac{z-w}{1-\bar{w} z}\right| .
> $$
> 
> (a) Prove that if $f: \mathbb{D} \rightarrow \mathbb{D}$ is holomorphic, then
> 
> $$
> \rho(f(z), f(w)) \leq \rho(z, w) \quad \text { for all } z, w \in \mathbb{D} .
> $$
> 
> Moreover, prove that if $f$ is an automorphism of $\mathbb{D}$ then $f$ preserves the pseudo-hyperbolic distance
> 
> $$
> \rho(f(z), f(w))=\rho(z, w) \quad \text { for all } z, w \in \mathbb{D} .
> $$
> 
> Hint: Consider the automorphism $\psi_\alpha(z)=(z-\alpha) /(1-\bar{\alpha} z)$ and apply the Schwarz lemma to $\psi_{f(w)} \circ f \circ \psi_w^{-1}$.

1.
$\psi_{\alpha}(z)= \frac{\alpha-z}{1-\bar{\alpha}z}$
 $\rho(f(z),f(w))\leq \rho(z,w)\iff \left| \psi_{f(w)}(f(z)) \right|\leq \left| \psi_{w}(z) \right|\iff \left| \psi_{f(w)}f(\psi_{w}(z)) \right|​\leq \left| z \right|$, since $\psi_{w}(z):\mathbb{D}\to \mathbb{D}$ is automorphism
 $g(z)=\psi_{f(w)}f(\psi_{w}(z))$, $g(0)=\psi_{f(w)}f(w)=0$
 And $g(z)$ is obviously holomorphic of $\mathbb{D}\to \mathbb{D}$
 $\left| g(z) \right|\leq \left| z \right|$ holds for $z\in \mathbb{D}$
 
 2.
 $g(z)=\psi_{f(w)}f(\psi_{w}(z))$ is also a automotphism. $\left| g(z) \right|\leq \left| z \right|,\left| g^{-1}(z) \right|\leq \left| z \right|\implies \left| g^{-1}(g(z)) \right|\leq |g(z)|\implies \left| g(z) \right|=\left| z \right|$
 Therefore, $\rho(f(z),f(w))=\rho(z,w)$

> [!Note] 3-b
> (b) Prove that
> 
> $$
> \frac{\left|f^{\prime}(z)\right|}{1-|f(z)|^2} \leq \frac{1}{1-|z|^2} \quad \text { for all } z \in \mathbb{D}
> $$
> 
> This result is called the Schwarz-Pick lemma. See Problem 3 for an important application of this lemma.

$f:\mathbb{D}\to \mathbb{D}$ holomorphic
$$
\left| \frac{f(z_{1})-f(z_{2})}{1-\overline{f(z_{1})}f(z_{2})} \right| \leq \left| \frac{z_{1}-z_{2}}{1-\overline{z_{1}}z_{2}} \right|
$$
Hence, we get
$$
\left| \frac{f(z_{1})-f(z_{2})}{z_{1}-z_{2}} \right| \leq \frac{\left| 1-\overline{f(z_{1})}f(z_{2}) \right| }{\left| 1-\overline{z_{1}}z_{2} \right| }
$$
Let $z_{1}\to z_{2}$, $\left| f'(z) \right|\leq \frac{\left| 1-\overline{f(z)}f(z) \right|}{|1-\bar{z}z|}=\frac{1-\left| f(z) \right|^{2}}{1-\left| z \right|^{2}}$
