>[!15.]
> Prove that for $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$,
>$$
\zeta(s)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)} \int_0^{\infty} \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1} d x
>$$
>[Hint: Write $1 /\left(e^x-1\right)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} e^{-n x}$.]

$$
I(s)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)} \int_0^{\infty} x^{s-1}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} e^{-n x}\right) d x
$$
设$\sigma=\mathrm{Re}(s)>1$. 我们下面考虑对积分和级数交换.
$\left| x^{s-1}\sum e^{-nx} \right|=\sum x^{\sigma-1}e^{-nx}$ 
在函数值较大时使用狄利克雷判别法控制: 存在$\delta$很大的时候, $x^{\sigma-1}e^{-\frac{n}{2}x}$ 在$[\delta,\infty)$上单调递减. 此时$x^{\sigma-1}e^{-\frac{n}{2}x}\rightrightarrows0,n\to \infty$. $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{-\frac{n}{2}x}$在$[\delta,\infty)$一致收敛. 所以$x\in[\delta,+\infty),\: \sum x^{\sigma-1}e^{-nx}$一致收敛.
当$x\in(0,\delta)$
$$
0\leq\frac{x^{\sigma-1}}{e^{x}-1}\leq \frac{x^{\sigma-1}}{x}=x^{\sigma-2}
$$
$$
\int_{0}^{\delta} x^{\sigma-2} \, dx \text{ 绝对可积}
$$
根据Lebesgue控制收敛定理, $(0,\delta)$上积分可交换
$$
I(s)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \int_{0}^{\infty} x^{s-1}e^{-nx} \, dx=\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{s}}\Gamma(s)=\zeta(s)
$$

> Use the previous exercise to give another proof that $\zeta(s)$ is continuable in the complex plane with only singularity a simple pole at $s=1$.
>[Hint: Write
>$$
\zeta(s)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)} \int_0^1 \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1} d x+\frac{1}{\Gamma(s)} \int_1^{\infty} \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1} d x
>$$
>The second integral defines an entire function, while
>$$
\int_0^1 \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1} d x=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{B_m}{m!(s+m-1)}
>$$
where $B_m$ denotes the $m^{\text {th }}$ Bernoulli number defined by
>$$
\frac{x}{e^x-1}=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{B_m}{m!} x^m .
>$$
>Then $B_0=1$, and since $z /\left(e^z-1\right)$ is holomorphic for $|z|<2 \pi$, we must have $\limsup _{m \rightarrow \infty}\left|B_m / m!\right|^{1 / m}=1 / 2 \pi$.]

$$
I_1(s)=\int_0^1 \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1} d x, \quad I_2(s)=\int_1^{\infty} \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1} d x
$$
对$I_{2}$
对每一个x, $F(s)=\frac{x^{s-1}}{e^{x}-1}$是全纯的. 
根据Morera定理, 如果下面这个积分可以交换, 那么就定义了整函数
$$
\int_{T}\int_{1}^{\infty} F(s,x) \, dx =\int_{1}^{\infty} \int_{T}F(s,x) \, dsdx=0 
$$
所以需要证明$\frac{x^{s-1}}{e^{x}-1}$是绝对可积的.
$$
\left| \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^{x}-1} \right| \leq \frac{x^{\left| s \right|-1 }}{e^{x}-1}\sim e^{-x}\text{ when }x\to +\infty
$$

对$I_{1}$
$$
\frac{1}{e^x-1}=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{B_m}{m!} x^{m-1}
$$
​	$$
\left|\frac{B_m}{m!} x^{s+m-2}\right| \leq\left|\frac{B_m}{m!}\right| x^{\sigma+m-2} 
$$
$x\in(0,1]$
$$
\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{\left|B_m\right|}{m!} x^m \leq \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{\left|B_m\right|}{m!}=C .
$$
假设$\mathrm{Re}s=\sigma>1$
$$
I_{1}(s)=\int_{0}^{1} \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{B_{m}}{m!}x^{s+m-2} \, dx =\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{B_{m}}{m!} \frac{1}{s+m-1}
$$
由于$I_{2}(s)$是整函数, 根据$I_{1}$积分表达式, 我们可以自然延拓到$\mathbb{C}-\{1\}$, 在$s=1$有一个单极点.
