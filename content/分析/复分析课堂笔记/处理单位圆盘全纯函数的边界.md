## 1. Extention holomorphic function from D to $\bar{D}$

>[! theorem: extention by uniform continuity]
>Let $f$ be a holomorphic function on $D\subset\Omega$, and $f$ is uniform continunous on $D$. Then $f$ can extend to $\bar{D}$.

### 1.1 Proof
Since uniform continuity, any $\{z_{n}\}$ that is Cauchy Series brings $\{f(z_{n})\}$ is Cauchy Series. Let $z_{n}\to \eta\in \partial D$. Let $f(\eta)=\lim_{n\to\infty}f(z_{n})$.
First, we prove $\forall \{\xi_{n}\}\subset D,\lim_{n\to\infty}\xi_{n}=\eta$ satissfies $\lim_{n\to\infty}f(\xi _{n})=f(\eta)$ 
Consider series $\xi_{1},z_{1},\xi_{2},z_{2},\dots$ converges to $\eta$. So n is lareg enough$\lvert \xi _{n}-z_{n}​	​	 \rvert<\delta$​	, $\lvert f(\xi_{n})-f(z_{n}) \rvert<\varepsilon$
Therefore, $\lim_{n\to\infty}f(\xi_{n})=\lim_{n\to\infty}f(z_{n})$. We have $\forall\varepsilon>0,\exists\delta \forall z\in D_{\delta}(\eta)\cap D_{r}(0),\lvert f(z)-f(\eta) \rvert<\varepsilon$ 
Second, we need to prove $\forall\varepsilon>0,\exists\delta>0,\forall z\in  D_{\delta}(\eta)\cap \partial D_{r}(0),\lvert f(z)-f(\eta) \rvert<\varepsilon$
If $\forall\varepsilon>0,\lvert z_{n}-z_{n}' \rvert<\delta\implies \lvert f(z_{n})-f(z_{n}') \rvert<\varepsilon$. Assume $w\in \partial D,\lvert w-\eta \rvert<\frac{\delta}{4}$ , we need to prove $|f(w)-f(\eta)|<\varepsilon$
Let $z_{n}\to w,z'_{n}\to \eta$ $\exists N,\forall n>N,\lvert z_{n}-z_{n}' \rvert< \frac{\delta}{2}\implies \lvert f(z_{n})-f(z_{n}') \rvert<\varepsilon$
$\lim_{n\to\infty}f(z_{n})=w,\lim_{n\to\infty}f(z_{n}')=\eta$. We have $\lvert f(z_{n})-f(w)\rvert<\varepsilon;\lvert f(z_{n}')-f(\eta)​	​	\rvert<\varepsilon$
So $\lvert f(\eta)-f(w) \rvert<3\varepsilon$

### 1.2 Proof Strategy
如果函数可以延拓到闭包, 那么这个函数是一致连续的(由于紧性). 所以这个条件本质上是一个充要条件.
一致连续性的作用: 一致连续性保证我们用不同的柯西序列逼近边界上的点, 得到的结果是相同的.
根据这个思想, 我们可以验证用柯西序列逼近出来的函数是良定义的, 然后再验证关于边界点是连续的.

## 2. ​	Schwarz Reflection Principle
>[!example] 
Suppose $f$ is a non-vanishing continuous function on $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$ that is holomorphic in $\mathbb{D}$. Prove that if
>$$
|f(z)|=1 \quad \text { whenever }|z|=1$$
then $f$ is constant.
[Hint: Extend $f$ to all of $\mathbb{C}$ by $f(z)=1 / \overline{f(1 / \bar{z})}$ whenever $|z|>1$, and argue as in the Schwarz reflection principle.]

Let $g(z)= \frac{1}{f\left( \frac{1}{z} \right)}$. $g(z)$ is holomorphic on $z\in D-\{0\}$ since it's a composition of holomorphic function.
$g(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}(z-z_{0})^{i}$ , for some $D_{r}(z_{0})$ 
$\overline{g(\bar{z})}=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\bar{a}_{i}(z-zo)^{i}$​	​	is holomorphic.
Observe $\overline{\frac{1}{f\left( \frac{1}{\bar{z}} \right)}}=f(z)$ when $\lvert z \rvert=1$. Then, we get the anwser.

使用最大模原理得, $\left| f(z) \right|\leq1,\forall z\in \mathbb{D}$. 由于没有零点, 根据最小模原理, $\inf_{z\in \mathbb{D}}\left| f(z) \right|\geq1$, 所以$\left| f(z) \right|\equiv1$ 

> [!18]
> Show that there is no holomorphic function $f$ in the unit disc $\mathbb{D}$ that extends continuously to $\partial \mathbb{D}$ such that $f(z)=1 / z$ for $z \in \partial \mathbb{D}$.

来源[[复分析习题11#18. 边值问题与唯一性]]
设$f$是全纯的, $g(z)=zf(z)$, 当$\left| z \right|=1$时, $g(z)=1$.

从而 $g(F(z))=1$, $\forall z\in \mathbb{R}$, 根据Schwarz反射原理, $g(F(z))$可以延拓到$\mathbb{C}$上, 使得在实轴上存在相等的聚点, 因此$g(F(z))\equiv1$, 也就是$g(z)\equiv1$, 但$f(z)= \frac{1}{z}$并不是在单位圆盘内全纯的, 矛盾.

# 3. 构造辅助函数得到结果

>[!习题课例子]
>$f(z)$在$0<r<\left| z-a \right|<R$上全纯, 且连续到边界, 且$f(Re^{i\theta}+a)=0,\:0\leq\theta<2\pi$.
>证明: 在圆环内$f(z)=0$

我们希望内圈也是0, 这样就能利用最大模原理. 但是从这里下手证明是非常困难的.
于是, 我们转而凑出一个函数, 让它们相乘能够让所有边界都是0.
设$g(z)=f(z)\cdot f\left( \frac{R}{z}r \right)$, $g(z)$是在圆环$r<\left| z-a \right|<R$上全纯, 且$g(Re^{i\theta}+a)=0,g(re^{i\theta}+a)=0$, 再根据最大模原理, $g(z)=0$, 所以$f(z)=0$
