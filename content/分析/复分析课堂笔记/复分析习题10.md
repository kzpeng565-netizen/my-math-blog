# 1. 围道积分与傅里叶变换

> [!3]
> 3. Show, by contour integration, that if $a>0$ and $\xi \in \mathbb{R}$ then
> $$
> \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{a}{a^2+x^2} e^{-2 \pi i x \xi} d x=e^{-2 \pi a|\xi|}
> $$
> and check that
> $$
> \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-2 \pi a|\xi|} e^{2 \pi i \xi x} d \xi=\frac{1}{\pi} \frac{a}{a^2+x^2}
> $$

设$\xi>0$, 选取下半平面来积分. 设$\gamma_{1}:R\to -R$, $\gamma_{2}:\theta\in[-\pi,\pi]\to R e^{i\theta}$
根据留数定理
$\int_{\gamma_{1}+\gamma_{2}}f(z)dz=2\pi i\text{Res}f$, $\text{Res}f=\lim_{z\to-ia} f(z)(z+ia)=-\pi e^{2\pi a\xi} /\pi$
$$
\int_{\gamma_{2}}f(z)dz= \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{\pi}^{2\pi} \frac{a}{a^{2}+R^{2}e^{2i\theta}}e^{-2\pi iR\xi e^{i\theta}} \, d\theta
$$
积分核的模长$\leq \frac{a}{\left| a^{2}+R^{2}e^{2i\theta} \right|}\leq \frac{a}{R^{2}-a^{2}}$
$$
\left| \int_{\gamma_{2}}f(z)dz \right| \leq \frac{\pi}{\pi} \frac{a}{R^{2}-a^{2}}\to0\quad \text{as }R\to \infty
$$
所以$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(z) \, dz=-\frac{-\pi e^{2\pi a\xi}}{\pi}=e^{2\pi a\xi}$
对于$\xi<0$, 选取上半平面同理.
$\xi=0$可以直接积分.

$I= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-2\pi a\left| \xi \right|}(\cos(2\pi x\xi)+i\sin(2\pi x\xi)) \, d\xi$ 由于sin是奇函数, $\cos$是偶函数
​	可以得到$I=2\int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-2\pi a\xi}\cos(2\pi \xi x) \, d\xi$
$$
\begin{align}
\int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-2\pi a}\cos(2\pi x\xi) \, dx =\int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-2\pi a\xi}\left(  \frac{\sin(2\pi x\xi)}{2\pi x} \right)' \, d\xi \\
=e^{-2\pi a\xi } \left.\frac{\sin(2\pi x\xi)}{2\pi x}\right|_{0}^{+\infty}+\int_{0}^{+\infty} ae^{-2\pi a\xi} \frac{\sin(2\pi x\xi)}{2x}  \, d\xi \\
=\dots \\
=\frac{a}{2\pi x^{2}}-\int_{0}^{+\infty} a^{2}e^{-2\pi a\xi} \frac{\cos(2\pi x\xi)}{2x^{2}} \, d\xi \\ \\ \\
\implies \frac{a}{2\pi x^{2}}- \frac{a^{2}}{x^{2}}I' =I' \\
\implies2I'= \frac{1}{\pi} \frac{a}{a^{2}+x^{2}}  
\end{align}
$$
对于$x=0$可以直接积分.

# 2. 泊松求和公式的应用

> [!7-a]
> (a) Let $\tau$ be fixed with $\operatorname{Im}(\tau)>0$. Apply the Poisson summation formula to
> $$
> f(z)=(\tau+z)^{-k}
> $$
> where $k$ is an integer $\geq 2$, to obtain
> $$
> \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(\tau+n)^k}=\frac{(-2 \pi i)^k}{(k-1)!} \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} m^{k-1} e^{2 \pi i m \tau}
> $$

​设$\hat{f}(z)=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} (\tau+z)^{-k}e^{-2\pi i\xi z} \, dz$
我们计算留数
$Resf= 2\pi i\frac{(e^{-2\pi i\xi z})^{(k-1)}|_{z=-\tau}}{(k-1)!}=2\pi i \frac{(-2\pi i\xi)^{k-1}}{(k-1)!}e^{2\pi i\xi \tau}$
- 对$\xi\geq0$, 我们考虑下半圆
$$
\begin{align}
-I= & \int_{\pi}^{2\pi} (\tau+Re^{i\theta})^{-k}e^{-2\pi i\xi Re^{i\Theta}}iRe^{i\theta} \, d\theta  \\
\left| I \right| \leq &  \int_{\pi}^{2\pi} \frac{e^{2\pi \xi R\sin\theta}R}{\left| \tau+Re^{i\theta} \right| ^{k}} \, d\theta \\
\leq  & \int_{\pi}^{2\pi} \frac{R}{\left| R-\tau \right| ^{k}} \, d\theta \\
 = & \frac{\pi R}{\left| R-r \right| ^{k}}\to0\quad \text{as }R\to \infty 
\end{align}
$$
因此, $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(z)e^{-2\pi i\xi x} \, dx=-\text{Res}f= \frac{(-2\pi i\xi)^{k}e^{2\pi i\xi \tau}}{(k-1)!}$ 
- 对$\xi<0$, 考虑上半圆
在半圆周上
$$
\left| I \right| \leq\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{e^{2\pi \xi R\sin\theta}R}{\left| \tau+Re^{i\theta} \right| ^{k}} \, d\theta 
$$
指数仍然小于0, 并且在正半平面上没有极点, 所以$\hat{f}(\xi)=0,\xi<0$
​	

> [!7-b]
> (b) Set $k=2$ in the above formula to show that if $\operatorname{Im}(\tau)>0$, then
> $$
> \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(\tau+n)^2}=\frac{\pi^2}{\sin ^2(\pi \tau)}
> $$

我们需要计算$-4\pi^{2}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}me^{2\pi i\tau m}$ 
$\left( \frac{e^{2\pi im\tau}}{2\pi i} \right)'=e^{2\pi im\tau}$
$$
\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}e^{2\pi im\tau}= \frac{e^{2\pi i\tau}}{1-e^{2\pi im\tau}}
$$
我们对这个式子求导( 幂级数在收敛圆内可以逐项求导 ) 或使用一致收敛的
$$
\frac{e^{2\pi i\tau}}{(1-e^{2\pi i\tau})^{2}}=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty} me^{2\pi i\tau m}
$$
LHS=$\frac{1}{-\sin ^{2}(\pi \tau)}$​	
从而$\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(\tau+n)^{2}}= \frac{\pi^{2}}{\sin ^{2}(\pi \tau)}$

> [!7-c]
> (c) Can one conclude that the above formula holds true whenever $\tau$ is any complex number that is not an integer?
> [Hint: For (a), use residues to prove that $\hat{f}(\xi)=0$, if $\xi<0$, and
> $$
> \left.\hat{f}(\xi)=\frac{(-2 \pi i)^k}{(k-1)!} \xi^{k-1} e^{2 \pi i \xi \tau}, \quad \text { when } \xi>0 .\right]
> $$

对于$\mathrm{Im}(\tau)<0$ 问题没有本质的变化, 因此只需要证明$\tau\in \mathbb{R}-\mathbb{Z}$
