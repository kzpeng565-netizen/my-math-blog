# 1. 傅立叶变换与三维波方程泊松公式

> [!Note] 题1
> 基于傅立叶变换和逆变换公式，从傅立叶观点出发重新推到三维波方程的泊松公式。

$$
\begin{cases}u_{t t}= \Delta u, & x \in \mathbb{R}^3, t>0 \\ u(x, 0)=\varphi(x), & x \in \mathbb{R}^3 \\ u_t(x, 0)=\psi(x), & x \in \mathbb{R}^3\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \widehat{u_{t t}}-\widehat{\Delta u}=0 \\
& \hat{u}_{t t}+\left(\xi_1^2+\xi_2^2+\xi_3^2\right) \hat{u}=0 . \\
& \hat{u}\left(\xi\right)=c_1 \cos \left(\xi_1^2+\xi_2^2+\xi_3^1\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} t+c_2 \sin \left(\xi_1^2+\xi_2^2+\xi_3^2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} t .
\end{aligned}
$$
初值为
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left.\hat{u}\right|_{t=0} & =\hat{\varphi}(\xi) \\
\left.\hat{u}_t\right|_{t=0} & =\hat{\psi}(\xi)
\end{aligned}
$$
​所以它的傅里叶变换的解为
$$
\widehat{u}(\xi)=\widehat{\varphi}(\xi)\cos \left| \xi \right| t+\frac{\widehat{\psi}(\xi)}{\left| \xi \right| }\sin \left| \xi \right| t
$$
我们需要计算它的傅里叶逆变换.

我们直接猜测$\widehat{F}(\xi,t)= \frac{\widehat{\psi}(\xi)}{\left| \xi \right|}\sin \left| \xi \right|t$的傅里叶逆变换为$\frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{\partial B(x,t)}\psi d\sigma=F(x,t)$ 
$$
\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \int_{\partial B(x, t)} \psi d \sigma e^{-i x \cdot \xi}=\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \int_{|y|=t} \psi(x-y) d y S_t
$$
我们对上式进行傅里叶变换
$$
\begin{aligned}
& I=\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \frac{1}{4 \pi  t} \int_{|y|=t} \psi(x-y) d y \dot{S}_t e^{-i x \cdot \xi} d x \\
& \left|\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \psi(x-y) e^{-i x \cdot \xi}\right|=\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \psi(y-x)
\end{aligned}
$$
被积函数假设为在$\mathbb{R}^{3}$​上绝对可积的, 则根据Fubini定理, 可以交换积分次序
$$
I=\int_{|y|=t} \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \frac{1}{4 \pi t} \psi(x-y) e^{-i x \cdot \xi} d x d_y S_t
$$
根据傅里叶变换的性质, 平移相当于乘上一个因子
$$
\begin{gathered}
\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \psi(x-y) e^{-i(x-y) \cdot \xi} e^{-i y \cdot \xi} d x \\
=\hat{\psi}(\xi) e^{-i y \cdot \xi}
\end{gathered}
$$
所以
$$
\begin{aligned}
I & =\int_{|y|=t} \frac{1}{4 \pi t} \hat{\psi}(\xi) e^{-i y \cdot \xi} d y S t \\
& =\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \hat{\psi}(\xi) \underbrace{\int_{|y|=t} e^{-i y \cdot \xi} d y S t}_{:=I_{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
只需计算$I_{2}$​的值
旋转坐标系，使Z轴对准$\xi$方向. $\left\{\begin{array}{l}y_1=r \sin \theta \cos \varphi \\ y_2=r \sin \theta \sin \varphi \\ y_3=r \cos \theta\end{array}\right.$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_2 &= \int_{|y|=t} e^{-i y \cdot \xi} \, dS_t 
     = \int_{0}^{2\pi} d\varphi \int_{0}^{\pi} e^{-i |\xi| t \cos\theta} \, t^2 \sin\theta \, d\theta \\
    &= 2\pi t^2 \int_{0}^{\pi} e^{-i |\xi| t \cos\theta} \sin\theta \, d\theta \\
    &= 2\pi t^2 \left[ \frac{e^{-i |\xi| t \cos\theta}}{i |\xi| t} \right]_{\theta=0}^{\theta=\pi} \\
    &= 2\pi t^2 \frac{2 \sin(|\xi| t)}{|\xi| t} \\
    &= 4\pi t \frac{\sin(|\xi| t)}{|\xi|}
\end{aligned}
$$
代入原式子得到
$$
\widehat{F}(\xi)=\widehat{\varphi}(\xi) \frac{\sin \left| \xi \right| t}{\left| \xi \right| }
$$

使用求导法则
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial t} F\right)}(x, t) & =\frac{\partial}{\partial t} \hat{F}(x, t) \\
& =\frac{\partial}{\partial t} \frac{\hat{\psi}(\xi)}{|\xi|} \sin |\xi| t \\
& =\hat{\psi}(\xi) \cos |\xi| t
\end{aligned}
$$
因此, 令$\psi'=\varphi$, 我们得到了$\hat{\varphi}(\xi)\cos \left| \xi \right|t$​	的傅里叶逆变换是$\frac{\partial }{\partial t} \frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{\partial B(x,t)}\varphi d\sigma$

# 3. 傅里叶级数系数收敛性引理

> [!Note] 引理3.2
> 设 $f(x)$ 是区间 $[0, l]$ 上的连续函数，它的 $m$ 阶导数连续，$m+1$ 阶导数分段连续，并且
> $$
> f^{(n)}(0)=f^{(n)}(l)=0 \quad\left(n=0,2, \cdots, 2\left[\frac{m}{2}\right]\right)
> $$
> 如果将 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[0, l]$ 上展开为傅里叶级数
> $$
> f(x) \sim \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k \sin \frac{k \pi x}{l},
> $$
> 则由系数 $a_k$ 所构成的级数 $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} k^m\left|a_k\right|$ 是收敛的．

> $$

> $$
> \left\{\begin{array}{l}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2}-a^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}=0, \\ u(0, t)=0, \quad \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(l, t)=0, \\ u(x, 0)=\frac{h}{l} x, \\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x, 0)=0 .\end{array}\right.
