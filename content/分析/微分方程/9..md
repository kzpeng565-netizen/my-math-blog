# 1. 傅立叶变换与三维波方程泊松公式

> [!Note] 题1
> 基于傅立叶变换和逆变换公式，从傅立叶观点出发重新推到三维波方程的泊松公式。

$$
\begin{cases}u_{t t}= \Delta u, & x \in \mathbb{R}^3, t>0 \\ u(x, 0)=\varphi(x), & x \in \mathbb{R}^3 \\ u_t(x, 0)=\psi(x), & x \in \mathbb{R}^3\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \widehat{u_{t t}}-\widehat{\Delta u}=0 \\
& \hat{u}_{t t}+\left(\xi_1^2+\xi_2^2+\xi_3^2\right) \hat{u}=0 . \\
& \hat{u}\left(\xi\right)=c_1 \cos \left(\xi_1^2+\xi_2^2+\xi_3^1\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} t+c_2 \sin \left(\xi_1^2+\xi_2^2+\xi_3^2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} t .
\end{aligned}
$$
初值为
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left.\hat{u}\right|_{t=0} & =\hat{\varphi}(\xi) \\
\left.\hat{u}_t\right|_{t=0} & =\hat{\psi}(\xi)
\end{aligned}
$$
​所以它的傅里叶变换的解为
$$
\widehat{u}(\xi)=\widehat{\varphi}(\xi)\cos \left| \xi \right| t+\frac{\widehat{\psi}(\xi)}{\left| \xi \right| }\sin \left| \xi \right| t
$$
我们需要计算它的傅里叶逆变换.

我们直接猜测$\widehat{F}(\xi,t)= \frac{\widehat{\psi}(\xi)}{\left| \xi \right|}\sin \left| \xi \right|t$的傅里叶逆变换为$\frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{\partial B(x,t)}\psi d\sigma=F(x,t)$ 
$$
\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \int_{\partial B(x, t)} \psi d \sigma e^{-i x \cdot \xi}=\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \int_{|y|=t} \psi(x-y) d y S_t
$$
我们对上式进行傅里叶变换
$$
\begin{aligned}
& I=\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \frac{1}{4 \pi  t} \int_{|y|=t} \psi(x-y) d y \dot{S}_t e^{-i x \cdot \xi} d x \\
& \left|\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \psi(x-y) e^{-i x \cdot \xi}\right|=\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \psi(y-x)
\end{aligned}
$$
被积函数假设为在$\mathbb{R}^{3}$​上绝对可积的, 则根据Fubini定理, 可以交换积分次序
$$
I=\int_{|y|=t} \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \frac{1}{4 \pi t} \psi(x-y) e^{-i x \cdot \xi} d x d_y S_t
$$
根据傅里叶变换的性质, 平移相当于乘上一个因子
$$
\begin{gathered}
\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \psi(x-y) e^{-i(x-y) \cdot \xi} e^{-i y \cdot \xi} d x \\
=\hat{\psi}(\xi) e^{-i y \cdot \xi}
\end{gathered}
$$
所以
$$
\begin{aligned}
I & =\int_{|y|=t} \frac{1}{4 \pi t} \hat{\psi}(\xi) e^{-i y \cdot \xi} d y S t \\
& =\frac{1}{4 \pi t} \hat{\psi}(\xi) \underbrace{\int_{|y|=t} e^{-i y \cdot \xi} d y S t}_{:=I_{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
只需计算$I_{2}$​的值
旋转坐标系，使Z轴对准$\xi$方向. $\left\{\begin{array}{l}y_1=r \sin \theta \cos \varphi \\ y_2=r \sin \theta \sin \varphi \\ y_3=r \cos \theta\end{array}\right.$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_2 &= \int_{|y|=t} e^{-i y \cdot \xi} \, dS_t 
     = \int_{0}^{2\pi} d\varphi \int_{0}^{\pi} e^{-i |\xi| t \cos\theta} \, t^2 \sin\theta \, d\theta \\
    &= 2\pi t^2 \int_{0}^{\pi} e^{-i |\xi| t \cos\theta} \sin\theta \, d\theta \\
    &= 2\pi t^2 \left[ \frac{e^{-i |\xi| t \cos\theta}}{i |\xi| t} \right]_{\theta=0}^{\theta=\pi} \\
    &= 2\pi t^2 \frac{2 \sin(|\xi| t)}{|\xi| t} \\
    &= 4\pi t \frac{\sin(|\xi| t)}{|\xi|}
\end{aligned}
$$
代入原式子得到
$$
\widehat{F}(\xi)=\widehat{\varphi}(\xi) \frac{\sin \left| \xi \right| t}{\left| \xi \right| }
$$

使用求导法则
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial t} F\right)}(x, t) & =\frac{\partial}{\partial t} \hat{F}(x, t) \\
& =\frac{\partial}{\partial t} \frac{\hat{\psi}(\xi)}{|\xi|} \sin |\xi| t \\
& =\hat{\psi}(\xi) \cos |\xi| t
\end{aligned}
$$
因此, 令$\psi'=\varphi$, 我们得到了$\hat{\varphi}(\xi)\cos \left| \xi \right|t$​	的傅里叶逆变换是$\frac{\partial }{\partial t} \frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{\partial B(x,t)}\varphi d\sigma$

# 2. 波动方程初值问题（傅立叶变换法）

> [!Note] 题8
> 解波动方程的初值问题
> $$
> \begin{cases}
> \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial t^{2}} -\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x^{2}}=t\sin x  & t>0,\:x\in \mathbb{R} \\
> u|_{t=0}=0,\quad \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} |_{t=0}=\sin x & (x\in \mathbb{R})
> \end{cases}
> $$

利用Duhamel Principle 计算

首先计算$u_{1}$是齐次性
$$
\begin{aligned}
u(x, t) & =\frac{1}{2} \int_{x-t}^{x+t} \sin s d s \\
& =\left.\frac{1}{2}(-\cos s)\right|_{x-t} ^{x+t} \\
& =\frac{1}{2}(-\cos (x+t)+\cos (x-t))
\end{aligned}
$$
非齐次项满足的方程为
$$
\begin{aligned}
& w_{t t}-\Delta w=0 \\
& \left.w\right|_{t=\tau}=\left.0 \quad w_t\right|_{t=\tau}=\tau \sin x
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\omega(x, t, \tau)=\frac{1}{2} \int_{x-(t-\tau)}^{x+(t-\tau)} \tau \sin s d s w(x, t, \tau)=\tau \sin x \sin (t-\tau)
$$
所以
$$
u(x,t)=u_{1}+\int_{0}^{t} w(x,t,\tau) \, d\tau=\sin x\sin t+(t\sin x-\sin x\sin t)=t\sin x
$$

# 3. 傅里叶级数系数收敛性引理

> [!Note] 引理3.2
> 设 $f(x)$ 是区间 $[0, l]$ 上的连续函数，它的 $m$ 阶导数连续，$m+1$ 阶导数分段连续，并且
> $$
> f^{(n)}(0)=f^{(n)}(l)=0 \quad\left(n=0,2, \cdots, 2\left[\frac{m}{2}\right]\right)
> $$
> 如果将 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[0, l]$ 上展开为傅里叶级数
> $$
> f(x) \sim \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k \sin \frac{k \pi x}{l},
> $$
> 则由系数 $a_k$ 所构成的级数 $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} k^m\left|a_k\right|$ 是收敛的．

对$f(x)$在$[-l,0]$奇延拓, 设$a_{k}$是傅里叶系数, $\widetilde{c_{k}}$是$f^{(m)}$的指数函数形式的三角级数.
如果$m$是奇数
$$
a_{k}^{(m+1)}=(-1)^{\frac{m+1}{2}}\left( \frac{k\pi}{l} \right)^{m+1}a_{k}
$$
如果$m$是偶数
$$
b_{k}^{(m+1)}=(-1)^{\frac{m}{2}}\left( \frac{k\pi}{l} \right)^{m+1}a_{k}
$$
由于$f^{(m+1)}$是分段连续函数, 在$L^{2}$​空间中傅里叶级数收敛到$f^{(m+1)}$.
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left| a_{k} ^{(m+1)}\right| ^{2}<\infty
$$
所以
$$

$$

# 4. 分离变量法求解波动方程混合问题

> [!Note] 题1-(1)
> 用分离变量法求下列问题的解：
> $$
> \left\{\begin{array}{l}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2}=a^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}, \\ \left.u\right|_{t=0}=\sin \frac{3 \pi x}{l},\left.\quad \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\right|_{t=0}=x(l-x), \\ u(0, t)=u(l, t)=0 ;\end{array}\right.
> $$

$$
\frac{w(x)}{w^{\prime \prime}(x)}=a^2 \frac{v(t)}{v^{\prime \prime}(t)}=\frac{1}{\lambda}
$$
- 对$w(x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \omega^{\prime \prime}(x)=\lambda \omega(x) \quad \omega(0)=\omega(l)=0 \\
& \Rightarrow \omega(x)=\sin \frac{n \pi}{l} x \quad \lambda=-\left(\frac{n \pi}{l}\right)^2
\end{aligned}
$$
- 对$v(t)$
$$
V(t)=A_n \cos \frac{a n \pi}{l} t+B_n \sin \frac{a n \pi}{l} t
$$
所以$u(x, t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(A_n \cos \frac{an \pi}{l} t+B_n \sin \frac{a n \pi}{L} t\right) \sin \frac{n \pi}{l} x$
代入边值条件
$u(x, 0)=\sin \frac{3 \pi x}{l} \Rightarrow A_1=A_2=A_4=\cdots=0, A_3=1$
另一方面
$$
\begin{gathered}
u_t^{\prime}(x, t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(-A_n \frac{a n \pi}{l} \sin \frac{a n \pi}{l} t+B_n \frac{a n \pi}{l} \cos \frac{a n \pi}{l} t\right) \sin \frac{n \pi}{l} x \\
u_t^{\prime}(x, 0)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} B_n \frac{a n \pi}{l} \sin \frac{n \pi}{l} x=x(l-x)
\end{gathered}
$$
对$x(l-x)$按照$\sin \frac{n\pi x}{l}$正交基展开
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{2}{l} \int_0^L x(l-x) \sin \frac{n \pi}{l} x d x \\
& =\frac{4}{l}\left(\frac{n \pi}{l}\right)^{-2} \int_0^l \sin \frac{n \pi}{l} x d x
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
B_n=8 \cdot(2 k-1)^{-4} l^3 \pi^{-4} \cdot a^{-1}
$$
**最终结果为**
$$
u(x,t)=- \frac{3a\pi}{l}\sin \frac{3a\pi t}{l}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 8 \cdot(2 k-1)^{-4} l^3 \pi^{-4} \cdot a^{-1} \frac{an\pi}{l}\sin \frac{n\pi x}{l}
$$

\begin{aligned}
w^{\prime \prime} & =-\lambda^2 w \\
w & =C_1 \cos \lambda x+C_{2}\sin \lambda x \\
& =C_2 \sin \lambda x \\
w^{\prime}(l) & =\left.C_2 \lambda \cos (\lambda x)\right|_{x=l} \\

\end{aligned}
$$

所以
$$
u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( A_{n}\cos \frac{a(1+2n)}{2l}t+B_{n}\sin \frac{a(1+2n)}{2l}t \right)\sin \frac{(1+2n)}{2l}x
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& u(x, 0)=w(x) v(0)=\frac{h}{l} x \\
& \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x, 0)=w(x) v^{\prime}(0)=0 .
\end{aligned}
$$
$$

$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_n= & (-1)^{n+1} \frac{2 h}{l^2}\left(\frac{2 l}{(1+2 n) \pi)}\right)^2 \\

\end{aligned}
$$
由第二个初值条件得$B_{n}\equiv0$
所以结果为
$$
u(x, t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n+1} \frac{8 h}{(1+2 \pi)^2 \pi^2} \cos \frac{a(1+2 \pi)}{2 l} t \sin \frac{(1+2 n)}{2 l} x
$$

# 5. 弹簧受迫振动问题

> [!Note] 题2
> 设弹簧一端固定，一端在外力作用下作周期振动，此时定解问题归结为
> $$
> \left\{\begin{array}{l}
> \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2}=a^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} \\
> u(0, t)=0, \quad u(l, t)=A \sin ^2 \omega t \\
> u(x, 0)=\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x, 0)=0
> \end{array}\right.
> $$

令$u=v+w$, $w$是满足线性插值条件的函数, 也就是
$$
w(x,t)=A \frac{x}{l}\sin ^{2}wt
$$
代入条件得到
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
V_{t t}-a^2 V_{x x}=-\frac{2 A w^2 x \cos 2 w t}{l} \\
V(0, t)=0 \quad V(l, t)=0 \\
V(x, 0)=V_t(x, 0)=0 .
\end{array}\right.
$$
对$T_{n}$用本征函数系展开
$$
\begin{gathered}
V(x, t)=\sum_n T_n(t) \sin \frac{n \pi x}{l} \\
\lambda_n=\frac{n \pi}{l}
\end{gathered}
$$
$$
\sum_n\left(T_n^{\prime \prime}+a^2 \lambda_n^2 T_n\right) \sin \lambda_n x=-2 A \omega^2 x \cos 2 \omega t
$$
对右边的式子进行本征函数系的展开
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
n \text { 为奇 } & T_n^{\prime \prime}+a^2 \lambda_n^2 T_n=0 \\
n \text { 为偶 } & T_n^{\prime \prime}+a^2 \lambda_n^2 T_n=-\frac{4 A w^2}{\lambda_n^2 L^2} n\pi \cos2wt
\end{array} .
$$

对于奇数, 显然$T_{n}\equiv0$
对于偶数
初值条件为$T_{n}'(0)=Tn$
