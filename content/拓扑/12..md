# 1. 约化同调与边缘算子

> [!Note] 1-1
> 如果我们改变 0 维边缘算子为
> $$
> \partial_0^*: S_0(X) \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z},
> $$
> 对任何 0 维奇异链 $\sum_{i=1}^k n_i x_i$， $\partial_0^*\left(\sum_{i=1}^k n_i x_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^k n_i \in \mathbb{Z}$ ．
> 证明：$\partial_0^* \partial_1=0$ ．

对于任意$\sigma:\Delta^{1}\to X$ 
$$
\partial_{1}(\sigma)=\sigma {\circ}F^{0}-\sigma {\circ}F^{1}
$$
$$
\partial_{0}^{*}(\sigma {\circ}F^{0}-\sigma {\circ}F^{1})=1-1=0
$$
所以对于任意的奇异单形满足=0, 对于奇异链自然满足

> [!Note] 1-2
> 定义
> $$
> \widetilde{H}_{i}(X)=
> \begin{cases}
> \frac{\ker\partial_{i}}{\operatorname{Im}\partial_{i+1}} & i\geq1 \\
> \frac{\ker\partial_{0}^{*}}{\operatorname{Im}\partial_{1}} & i=0
> \end{cases}
> $$
> 证明
> $$
> H_{i}(X)\cong
> \begin{cases}
> \widetilde{H}_{i}(X) & i\geq1 \\
> \widetilde{H}_{i}(X)\oplus \mathbb{Z} & i=0
> \end{cases}
> $$


对于$i\geq1$ 结论显然.

对于$i=1$ , 不妨设$X$ 有$k$个连通分支, 这等价于$H_{0}(X)=\{n_{1}x_{1}+\dots+n_{k}x_{k}\}$, $x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{k}$是$k$个互不相同的代表元.
由于对于任意的0维奇异复形, 它的边缘都是0, 所以$ker\partial_{0}=S_{0}(X)$ , 所以$S_{0}(X) / \mathrm{Im}\partial_{1}=\{n_{1}x_{1}+\dots+n_{k}x_{k}\}$.

考虑$ker\partial^{*}_{0} / \mathrm{Im}\partial_{1}\subset S_{0}(X) /\mathrm{Im}\partial_{1}$  
$$
\widetilde{H}_{1}(X)=\{n_{1}x_{1}+\dots+n_{k}x_{k}\mid n_{1}+\dots+n_{k}=0\}
$$
所以$n_{k}=-(n_{1}+\dots+n_{k-1})$ , 这给出
$$
\widetilde{H}_{1}(X)\cong\bigoplus _{i=1}^{k-1}\mathbb{Z}
$$

**更通用的方法: 如果连通分支无穷**

定义同态$\varepsilon:H_{0}(X)\to \mathbb{Z};\:\sum_{k=1}^{m}n_{k}\sigma _k\to \sum_{k=1}^{m}n_{k}$ , 显然是满同态.
不妨记$\sigma_{k}=x_{k}$ 

我们选取基点$x_{0}$ , 对于任意$[c]\in H_{0}(X)$
$$
c=c-\varepsilon(c)x_{0}+\varepsilon(c)x_{0}
$$
则$\varepsilon(c-\varepsilon(c)x_{0})=0$ , 从而属于$\widetilde{H}_{0}(X)$

定义群同态$\varphi:H_{0}(X)\to \widetilde{H}_{0}(X)\bigoplus\mathbb{Z}$ 
$$
c\to(c-\varepsilon(c)x_{0},\varepsilon(c)x_{0})
$$
根据线性性质, 显然是群同态
它的逆映射$\varphi ^{-1}:(c-\varepsilon(c)x_{0},\varepsilon(c)x_{0})\to c-\varepsilon(c)x_{0}+\varepsilon(c)x_{0}$
显然是群同态. 所以$\varphi$是同构
# 2. 连通分支与同调群分解

> [!Note] 2
> 设 $X$ 有两个道路连通分支 $X_1, X_2$ ，证明：$q$ 维奇异链群
> $$
> S_q(X) \cong S_q\left(X_1\right) \oplus S_q\left(X_2\right)
> $$
> 且
> $$
> \partial_q\left(S_q\left(X_1\right)\right) \subset S_{q-1}\left(X_1\right), \quad \quad \partial_q\left(S_q\left(X_2\right)\right) \subset S_{q-1}\left(X_2\right) .
> $$
> 因而 $H_q(X) \cong H_q\left(X_1\right) \oplus H_q\left(X_2\right)$ ．

因为$X_{1},X_{2}$​	不连通, 因此$\sigma:\Delta^{q}\to X$ 的像只能包含在其中一个当中, 否则$\sigma ^{-1}(X_{1}),\sigma ^{-1}(X_{2})$ 既开又闭非空
因此, 
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{m} n_{k}\sigma_{k}=\sum_{k=1}^{s} n_{k}\sigma^{1}_{k}+\sum_{k=s+1}^{m} n_{k}\sigma^{2}_{k}
$$
$\sigma^{i}$代表像位于$X_{i}$的奇异单形, 因此我们定义
$$
S_{q}(X)\to S_{q}(X_{1})\oplus S_{q}(X_{2})
$$
根据线性性质, 显然是同态. 单射满射显然.

对于$\sigma^{i}$ , 它的面是$\sigma^{i}{\circ}F^{i}$, 像包含于$\sigma^{i}(\Delta^{q})\subset X_{i}$当中 因此是$X_{i}$的奇异q-1单形, 所以
$\partial_q\left(S_q\left(X_1\right)\right) \subset S_{q-1}\left(X_1\right), \quad \quad \partial_q\left(S_q\left(X_2\right)\right) \subset S_{q-1}\left(X_2\right)$

简记$\partial_{q}(S_{q}(X_{i}))=\partial_{q}(X_{i})$, 

设$c_{1}\in \partial_{q}(X_{1}),\:c_{2}\in \partial_{q}(X_{2})$, 定义
$$
\phi:H_{q}(X_{1})\oplus H_{q}(X_{2})\to H_{q}(X),\:([c_{1}],[c_{2}])\to [c_{1}]+[c_{2}]
$$
假设$c_{1}'$与$c_{1}$只相差一个$B_{q}(X_{1})$, $c_{2}'$与$c_{2}$同样相差边缘, 
$$
\begin{aligned}
& c_1-c_1^{\prime} \in \operatorname{Im} \partial_{q+1}\left(X_1\right) \\
& c_2-c_2^{\prime} \in \operatorname{Im} \partial_{q+1}\left(X_2\right) \\
& c_1^{\prime}+c_2^{\prime}-\left(c_1+c_2\right) \in \operatorname{Im} \partial_{q+1}(X)
\end{aligned}
$$
因此是良定义的.

- 单射
由于$S_{q}(X_{1})\cap S_{q}(X_{2})=0$, 有$H_{q}(X_{1})\cap H_{q}(X_{2})=0$ 
如果$[c_{1}]+[c_{2}]=0$, 则$[c_{1}]=0,[c_{2}]=0$

- 满射
任取$[c]\in H_{q}(X)$ , 根据前面的同构, $c=c_{1}+c_{2}$, 还需要证明$c_{1}\in \partial_{q}(X_{1}),\:c_{2}\in \partial_{q}(X_{2})$ 
$$
\partial_{q}c=0\implies \partial_{q}(c_{1})+\partial_{q}(c_{2})=0
$$
而$\partial_{q}(c_{1})\subset S_{q-1}(X_{1}),\partial_{q}(c_{2})\subset S_{q-1}(X_{2})$ 二者相交=0, 所以$\partial_{q}(c_{1})=\partial_{q}(c_{2})=0$ 属于闭链群
# 3. 逆道路与边缘

> [!Note] 3
> 记 $\sigma^{-1}: \Delta^1 \rightarrow X$ 是 $\sigma$ 的逆道路．证明：1 维奇异链 $\sigma+\sigma^{-1}$ 是一个边缘．

$$
\begin{align}
\partial(\sigma+\sigma ^{-1})=\partial(\sigma)+\partial(\sigma ^{-1}) \\
=\sigma {\circ}F^{0}-\sigma {\circ}F^{1}+\sigma ^{-1}{\circ}F^{0}-\sigma ^{-1}{\circ}F^{1} \\
=\sigma {\circ}F^{0}-\sigma {\circ}F^{1}-\sigma {\circ}F^{0}+\sigma {\circ}F^{1} =0
\end{align}
$$
所以$\sigma+\sigma ^{-1}\in Z_{1}(X)$
